# Introduction

Welcome to the **Community Network Handbook** — a practical guide to building and running community networks from scratch, even with limited resources.

This chapter explains **why** this handbook exists and **who** it's written for. If you're eager to get your hands dirty, feel free to skip ahead to [Chapter 2](../2-Imaginary-Use-Case/index.md) — but we recommend spending five minutes here first.

## How this book is organized

The handbook is built around **two tracks that mirror each other**. The story shows you *what* to build and *why*; the guide shows you *how*. Read across them as you go.

1. **Introduction** — You are here. Motivation and context.
2. **Imaginary Use Case** *(the story)* — A fictional but realistic journey that walks you through building a community network step by step, from the first router to a full deployment. Each section is a challenge you'll face.
3. **Guide** *(the how-to)* — Detailed, step-by-step technical recipes for every technology mentioned in the story. Self-contained: open the one you need.
4. **Real Use Cases** — Field deployments where these ideas have been applied for real, including our network at a primary school in [Namibia](../4-Real-Use-Cases/index.md).

!!! tip "How to read this book"
    **New here?** Read Chapter 2 straight through to build a mental model, then dip into Chapter 3 when you're ready to build. **In a hurry?** Skim the story and jump to the exact Chapter 3 recipe you need. Not sure which describes you? See [who this is for](1.2-Target-Reader.md).
